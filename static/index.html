        4 Core Research Themes & Design Responses

        Theme 1: Feedback Transparency

        Gap: Existing AI systems score without explaining why.
        Response: Rubric-grounded transparent evaluation + 'Why This Feedback?' evidence audit.

        Theme 2: Longitudinal Profile

        Gap: Personalization is usually role-locked or single session.
        Response: Persistent database tracking recurring weaknesses across practice days.

        Theme 3: Reflection Loop

        Gap: Systems rarely invite structured reflection before retry.
        Response: Metacognitive guided reflection + Attempt 1 vs 2 comparative delta diffs.

        Theme 4: Trust & Bias Resistance

        Gap: Blackbox scoring invites bias and LLM sycophancy.
        Response: Fixed, auditable criteria (Relevance, Specificity, Evidence, Structure).
